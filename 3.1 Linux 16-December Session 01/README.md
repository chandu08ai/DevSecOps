**Waterfall:**
SMS - School Management System

**100 years back:** --> Waterfall Model 
- only Final exam
- Stakeholders:
  - Teachers --> Not serious to complete syllabus from Day-1
  - Parents --> Yes, worried about whether they pass or not
  - Students --> Not serious to study from Day-1
- Result: pass percentage is 30% 
Process Change:
- UNIT TEST - 1, 2, 3, 4, Quaterly, Half-Yearly, Pre-Final, Final
- UNIT TEST-1 --> 30 days 
- Stakeholders are same
  - Teachers: They should be serious from DAY-1 to complete syllabus for UNIT TEST-1
  - Students: study 1 week before UNIT TEST-1
  - Parents: they are waiting for UNIT TEST-1 results
- Results: pass percentage is 80%

**Comarision between SMS and DevOps:**
- Parents --> Clients
- Students --> Developers
- Teachers --> IT Management
